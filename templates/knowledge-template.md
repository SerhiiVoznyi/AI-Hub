# Summary

Describe the durable concept or shared contract in one short paragraph.

## Use When

- Situations where agents or prompts should link here instead of restating content.

## Content

1. Keep the document dense and reference-oriented.
2. Prefer imperative bullets and keyed tables over essays.
3. Point to sibling knowledge docs for adjacent concerns; do not duplicate them.

## Notes

- Do not paste this document’s body into prompts; instruct the runtime to load the path.
- Navigation belongs in `knowledge/README.md`, not in frontmatter.
