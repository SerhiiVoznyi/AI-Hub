# AI-Hub Context

AI-Hub is a repository of reusable AI artifacts organized by competency rather than by vendor or model: agents, skills, prompts, workflows, and the shared contracts they depend on.

Browse: [../README.md](../README.md) · Canonical rules: [../AI.md](../AI.md) · Delivery roles: [../agents/README.md](../agents/README.md)

Priorities:

- keep artifacts small, composable, and easy to cross-link
- state executor requirements as capabilities, not as product names
- keep role definitions in `agents/` and tool wiring in thin adapters
- avoid adding new top-level categories until content volume justifies them
